# Accessibility & Ergonomics Guardian

Ensures every layout (Sidebar, Top Navbar, Bottom Dock, Zen Focus) and theme (Classic, Neon, Candy, Nordic, Arcade, OLED, Sunset) adheres to strict usability standards.

## Audit Checklist:
1. **Color Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA)**:
   - Minimum 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text across ALL skins.
   - Special attention to OLED (pure black `#000000`) and Arcade high-saturation neon.
2. **Keyboard Ergonomics**:
   - Number shortcuts `1-4` for Sprint answers.
   - `Enter` to advance or submit dialogs.
   - `Escape` to close modals, drawers, and zen menu.
   - Visible, distinct `:focus-visible` outlines on all interactive elements.
3. **Mobile & Touch Targets**:
   - Minimum 44x44px touch targets on Bottom Command Dock and mobile navigation buttons.
   - Custom checkboxes and radio buttons styled with accessible touch boundaries.
